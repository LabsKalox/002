# BankMaster 2.0 — prompts para o Veo 3

Mesmo formato do KaloxSooth: o apresentador no estúdio preto fala com a câmera, e a tela real do plugin
entra entre as falas. O Veo gera só o apresentador. A interface do BankMaster vem da sua gravação de tela,
porque o Veo inventaria botões que não existem.

**Para manter o mesmo personagem e o mesmo estúdio:** em todos os clipes use
**Frames to Video** com `assets/frame-inicial-estudio-preto.png` como quadro inicial, formato 9:16, com áudio.

Roteiro total: cerca de 22 s em 3 clipes de 8 s.

---

## Clipe 1 — Gancho (0–8 s)

```
Vertical selfie video, handheld phone front camera, natural and lightly moving.
A Brazilian music producer in his early thirties, black hoodie, sits in his all-black
futuristic music studio at night (matte black ribbed acoustic panels, thin recessed icy
white LED light lines, curved monitor glowing softly behind him, icy rim light on his hair
and shoulders).

He looks straight into the lens with a slightly frustrated, knowing expression at first,
like he is calling out a struggle every producer knows, then his face lights up with
confidence.

0-3s: he shakes his head slightly and raises his eyebrows, empathetic.
3-6s: he leans a little closer to the camera, small smile, raising one finger as if about to
reveal a secret.
6-8s: he points down toward the bottom of the frame twice, excited.

Dialogue, in Brazilian Portuguese, casual and energetic male voice:
"Cê passa horas tentando deixar tua master alta e ela continua fraca? Olha isso aqui."

Audio: only his voice and quiet room tone. No music.
No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.
```

## Clipe 2 — Revelação (8–16 s)

```
Vertical selfie video, handheld phone front camera, natural and lightly moving.
Same Brazilian music producer, black hoodie, same all-black futuristic studio at night
(matte black ribbed panels, thin icy white LED lines, curved monitor behind him, icy rim light).

He speaks with proud, contagious excitement, like he is showing off something he built himself.

0-3s: he gestures to himself with an open hand on his chest, proud.
3-5s: he mimes choosing something with one finger, tapping the air once.
5-8s: he leans back relaxed, opens both palms and shrugs with a confident smile, as if
saying "that's all it takes".

Dialogue, in Brazilian Portuguese, casual and energetic male voice:
"Esse é o BankMaster 2.0, meu plugin novo. Cê só escolhe o gênero... e espera."

Audio: only his voice and quiet room tone. No music.
No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.
```

Pronúncia: se o Veo errar o nome, troque no diálogo por `"Bénk Master dois ponto zero"`.

## Clipe 3 — Chamada final (16–24 s)

```
Vertical selfie video, handheld phone front camera, natural and lightly moving.
Same Brazilian music producer, black hoodie, same all-black futuristic studio at night
(matte black ribbed panels, thin icy white LED lines, curved monitor behind him, icy rim light).

He speaks directly to the viewer with a playful, inviting energy, like he is letting a friend
in on something before everyone else.

0-4s: he nods with conviction, small satisfied smile.
4-7s: he points at the camera, then makes a quick "write" gesture with his fingers.
7-8s: he winks and smiles.

Dialogue, in Brazilian Portuguese, casual and energetic male voice:
"Ele faz a master pra você. Tá chegando: comenta MASTER que eu te aviso primeiro."

Audio: only his voice and quiet room tone. No music.
No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text.
```

Vertical cinematic shot, slow push-in. A tired young Brazilian music producer in a black

## Dicas
- Gere de 2 a 4 versões de cada clipe e escolha pela boca sincronizada e pela naturalidade.
- Se o Veo puser legenda na imagem, gere de novo.
- **Grave a tela do BankMaster** (no Mac: Cmd+Shift+5; no Windows: Win+Alt+R) escolhendo um gênero,
  clicando e esperando até o fim. É isso que entra entre o Clipe 1 e o Clipe 2.
- Se quiser usar a sua voz, grave as três falas no celular. Eu troco e sincronizo com a boca, como no KaloxSooth.
- "Comenta MASTER que eu te aviso" só funciona se você responder os comentários (na mão ou com o ManyChat).
